The process

From photo to diagnosis-ready read, in three steps.

No manual grading, no waiting room — just a fundus photo and a clinically-mapped output you can act on.

STEP 01

Upload a fundus photo

Any standard retinal camera image, uploaded directly from your device — JPG or PNG.

STEP 02

Retina is isolated

The image is cropped to the retina and contrast-enhanced with CLAHE before grading.

STEP 03

Stage & confidence

An EfficientNetB0 model returns a DR stage with a confidence percentage and next steps.

Mapped to all five clinical DR stages.

Each read lands on one of the five recognised stages of diabetic retinopathy, so the result speaks the same language as your ophthalmologist.
