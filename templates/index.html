RetinaScan
Diabetic Retinopathy Screening

Read a fundus image in seconds, not in a waiting room.

Upload a retinal fundus photograph and the model grades it across the five clinical stages of diabetic retinopathy — from No DR to Proliferative DR — with a confidence score for each read.

Upload a fundus image
JPG or PNG — a clear, centred retinal photograph works best

Add a fundus photo

Any standard retinal camera image, uploaded directly from your device.

02 — PREPROCESS
Retina is isolated

The image is cropped to the retina and contrast-enhanced (CLAHE) before grading.

03 — GRADE
Stage & confidence

An EfficientNetB0 model returns a DR stage with a confidence percentage.
